A visual record of Benue State’s democratically elected governors. Since civilian government began in 1979, each elected governor of the state has been of Tiv extraction.

This collection covers democratically elected civilian governors, not the military administrators who governed Benue during periods of military rule.

Names, terms and portraits are based on the Benue State Government’s Present & Past Leaders archive. Historical context was cross-checked against the public list of Benue State governors.
